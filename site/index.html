02 / How we think

Better models deserve
better ways of working.

The model will change. Your standards—and the work you’ve already done—should carry forward.

  1. 01

    Choice at the center.

    Use the intelligence that fits the task. Keep the workflow portable through explicit interfaces and files you can take with you.

  2. 02

    Evidence over confidence.

    A persuasive answer still needs support. Keep facts, assumptions, risks, and recommendations distinct. Make room for “we don’t know yet.”

  3. 03

    Checks that mean something.

    Let tests and evidence set the bar. A failed check should explain the next useful step; a model saying “done” should not settle it.

  4. 04

    Work that outlasts a chat.

    Preserve the context, sources, settings, and completed work. Pick it up again without rebuilding the whole story.
